// privacy

Privacy policy

Claude Links does not collect, send or sell any data. Everything it uses stays in your browser.

What the extension stores

Only the configuration you enter in the popup: page domains and rules (key, value and folder). It lives in the browser’s chrome.storage.sync, which the browser itself may sync across your devices when you are signed in. The extension has no server and sends this configuration nowhere.

What it reads on pages

It runs only on the domains you added and authorized. On those pages, it looks in the visible text for snippets that match your rules and turns them into links. Page text is neither stored nor sent.

When you click a link

The claude:// link is handed to the operating system, which opens the Claude Desktop installed on your computer with the message filled in. From then on, Claude’s privacy policy applies. The extension takes no part in that conversation.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
